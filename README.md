## 📺 Overview
*Stranger Things* is a science fiction, mystery, and horror series created by the Duffer Brothers.  
It is set in the 1980s in the small town of Hawkins, Indiana, where a young boy mysteriously disappears.  
His friends, family, and a telekinetic girl named **Eleven** uncover government secrets and a terrifying alternate dimension known as **The Upside Down**.

---

## 🌟 Why I Like It
- Nostalgic 80s atmosphere with great music  
- Strong friendships and teamwork among the main characters  
- Thrilling mix of mystery, sci-fi, and emotional storytelling  
- Amazing performances and cinematography 🎬

## 🔍 Interesting Facts
1. The show is inspired by 1980s movies like *E.T.*, *The Goonies*, and *Stand by Me*.  
2. It was almost called **“Montauk”** before being renamed *Stranger Things*.  
3. The show’s theme music was created using synthesizers for that retro 80s vibe.
